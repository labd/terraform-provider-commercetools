## v1.23.0 - 2026-10-22
### Added
* New resource `commercetools_insights_configuration` to manage the Platform Insights configuration of a Project, exporting logs and metrics to NewRelic, Dynatrace, Datadog or an OTLP provider
* Enhance `commercetools_api_extension` to support the `dependencies`, `expansion_paths` and `additional_context` fields. Dependencies can be referenced by key or by id.
### Fixed
* Fix `commercetools_channel` reading `address.additional_street_info` from the API's `additionalAddressInfo` field instead of `additionalStreetInfo`
